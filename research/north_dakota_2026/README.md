- General: https://vip.sos.nd.gov/candidatelist.aspx?eid=348

Scope: U.S. House, partisan statewide offices, State Senate (odd districts, single-member), State House (odd districts, two members each) and unexpired-term House seats in Districts 20, 26 and 42 (one seat each). Out of scope: judicial, county, soil conservation, Garrison Diversion, and the nonpartisan Superintendent of Public Instruction (not a party nomination; general race already exists in statewide_races_2026.yml and was not given candidacies here).

Counts: 305 Candidacies, 170 Persons, 151 Offices, 151 Races (100 primary nominations), 51 Districts, 51 Elections, 2 Parties (Republican, Democratic-NPL; "independent nomination" = petition line), 23 verified website Channels.

Federal records reused: Fedorchak, Hammer, Neville, Tuttle. Name fields come from the SoS first/middle/last columns. 9 cross-state name-code collisions, all minted --new.
