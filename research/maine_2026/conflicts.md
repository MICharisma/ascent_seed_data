## Withdrawals

- Lucien JB Daigle, SR 1, R (2/9). Per the SoS he is now a candidate for SS 1; he is not on the 3/16 primary list for either office.
- Nicole Cummings Bambrick, SR 77; Lisa M. Nelthropp, SR 113; Joshua K. Morris, SS 17; Louis Larios, SR 51. All withdrew 3/31 and are classed before-primary-ballot. The ballot certification date was not confirmed; the classification relies on the withdrawal date.

- Brian R. Pellerin (York County Sheriff) remains on the ballot after a late withdrawal; out of scope.
